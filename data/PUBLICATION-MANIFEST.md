## Provenance caveat

The `captures/` files are original sigrok `.sr` containers produced by
the SME. They are published **byte-for-byte as acquired**, including a
known-wrong channel label on CH7 ("PMCYC" where the physical net is
DACK). That label is preserved deliberately: it is evidence of what the
acquisition software was told. The correction is applied at analysis
time, never by editing a capture. See `docs/probe-map.md`.
